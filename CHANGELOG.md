# Changelog

Pulseline releases. Downloads: [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/hkboujrida/pulseline/releases).

## 0.3.0

- **Right now** on Today, on demand: live heart rate and trace, current heart-rate
  zone, movement (still / light / moving / very active, from wrist ENMO with
  Hildebrand et al. 2014 cut points), today's strain refreshed by a sync every
  10 minutes, a one-minute "HRV right now" spot check, and band connection, wear and
  battery.
- Off by default; stops after 15 minutes, on tab switch or when the app is
  backgrounded.

## 0.2.0

- Full redesign of all four tabs.
- Today: readiness hero ring, then Sleep / Strain / Steps / Overnight vitals cards.
- Vitals: hero night (sleep ring against need, stage split), readiness against your
  usual, overnight vitals against your usual range.
- Activity: "This week" card (workouts, form, week dots), forecast moved up.
- My Health: 30-day trend tiles and a monthly report card.

## 0.1.0

- First release as Pulseline (renamed from an Edge fork, new icon, own app id
  `dev.pulseline.app`).
- New look: near-black / warm-white surfaces, flat cards, lighter numerals.
- Tabs: Today · Vitals · Activity · My Health.
- New: readiness against your usual, overnight vitals against your usual range,
  7-day training-load forecast, strength progress (records, estimated 1RM, weekly
  volume), monthly report, "More for you" shortcuts.
